Don't Slip!


The biggest hazard during the winter is slipping on ice. The University of Calgary prioritizes primary walkways, wheelchair ramps, building entrances and fire exits to make sure that they are clean during the winter. However, the University also provides the students with sand near the doors incase of missed slippery ice patches.
